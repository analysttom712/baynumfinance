What each number tells you

  • Cost per mile is your floor. Take a load below it and you lose money on every mile.
  • Profit per mile is what you keep. If it shrinks month to month, something is creeping up.
  • Deadhead is empty miles. They cost the same as loaded miles but pay nothing.
  • Factoring fees (gold bar) are easy to miss because they come out before you're paid.
