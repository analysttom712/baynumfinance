About Me

I'm Thomas Baynum, owner of Baynum Financial & Analytics, LLC. By day, I work in benefits at a financial institution, where I've served thousands of individuals by reconciling their accounting activity. Every number has to tie out, and getting it there is my kind of puzzle. I built the Power BI reporting I use to keep it all in balance.

I'm also a certified Intuit QuickBooks ProAdvisor, at the Gold tier.

Industry experience

  • Financial services: benefits and payroll reconciliation
  • Government accounting: fiscal analyst for a South Carolina state agency
  • Food service: assistant general manager
  • Transportation and logistics: truck driver and owner-operator, running my own business on the road

I didn't start behind a desk. I know what it's like to be on the other side of the books: working long days, then trying to figure out at the end of the week where the money went.

Why I do this

My passion is data-driven solutions: taking numbers that feel overwhelming and turning them into something easy to understand. Finances can sound tricky. My goal is for you to feel confident in your books, so when tax time comes, you and your tax preparer have clean, reconciled numbers to work from.

What I offer

  • Recording and categorizing transactions
  • Monthly bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Simple monthly reports that are easy to understand

Available at special pricing:

  • Custom visual dashboards, built around each client's business
  • Financial analysis: gross and net profit margins, liquidity ratios, inventory turnover, and more

What I bring is the training, the certification, and hands-on reconciliation experience, applied carefully to your books.

Who I work with

Small to medium businesses in South Carolina, and remotely wherever you are. My background in food service and trucking means I already speak the language of restaurants, logistics, and owner-operated businesses.

At this time, I'm not able to take on clients in the agriculture industry.

How I work

New projects always excite me, but what matters most is building a lasting relationship: a client who knows they can trust me and my services, and who understands their numbers better because we worked through them together.
