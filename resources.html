1. Getting started

Welcome! Here's what happens in your first two weeks:

  1. Engagement letter. I'll send a short agreement covering your services, monthly fee, and what each of us is responsible for. Please sign and return it.
  2. QuickBooks access. If you already use QuickBooks Online, add me as your accountant (see step 2). If you don't, I'll set up a new company file for you.
  3. Gather your records. Bank and credit card statements, prior tax return, any loan paperwork, and your sales/POS reports. Use the secure upload link I send you, not email.
  4. Kickoff call. We'll walk through your accounts, how you get paid, and what reports you want each month.
2. Add me as your accountant in QuickBooks

This takes about two minutes. You need to be the primary admin or a company admin on your QuickBooks account.

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online.
  2. Click the gear icon (⚙️) at the top right and select Manage users.
  3. Open the Accounting firms tab and click Invite.
  4. Enter Tom Baynum and tom@baynumfinancial.com.
  5. Click Send.

You stay in control: you can remove my access at any time from that same screen.

3. Sending documents securely

I'll send you a secure upload link when we start working together. Please use it for anything financial.

  • Statements, receipts, invoices, and reports: use the secure link.
  • Photos of paper receipts are fine. Make sure the date, vendor, and total are readable.
  • Label files with the month, for example 2026-10 Bank Statement.pdf.

Please never email Social Security numbers, full bank or card account numbers, or copies of your ID. I'll only ask for what's actually needed for your books.

4. Monthly checklist

To keep your books current, please send these by the 5th of each month for the month before:

  • Any bank or credit card account that isn't connected to QuickBooks: the monthly statement.
  • Receipts for cash purchases.
  • Your POS end-of-month sales report (if your POS isn't synced to QuickBooks).
  • Anything new: a loan, a new vendor, equipment purchases, or changes in how you get paid.
  • Answers to any questions I've sent about unclear transactions.

I'll send your monthly reports (Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet) once the month is reconciled.

5. POS & inventory

If you sell products, your point-of-sale system should do the heavy lifting:

  • Track every item separately, including each size, flavor, or variant, so counts and costs are accurate.
  • Enter your cost for each item when you receive stock. This is how we calculate your real profit margin.
  • Count your inventory at least once a quarter and fix any differences in the POS.
  • Connect your POS to QuickBooks if it supports it. I can set up and map the connection for you.

Not sure which POS fits your business, or need it set up and connected? That's a service I offer. Just ask.
