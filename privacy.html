The short version

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. I don't sell, rent, or trade anyone's information. Client financial records are handled separately from this website and are kept confidential.

What this website collects

This site has no sign-up forms, accounts, or checkout. The only data collected automatically comes from Google Analytics, which uses cookies to gather general information such as:

  • Pages viewed, time on the site, and how far you scroll
  • How you arrived (for example, a search engine, LinkedIn, or a direct link)
  • General location (city or region), device type, and browser
  • Whether you clicked an email or phone link. Your email address and phone number are not recorded.

I use this information only to understand how the site is used and to improve it. It isn't used to identify you personally.

When you contact me

If you email or call, I receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, and details about your business. I use it only to respond to you and, if we work together, to provide services. I don't add you to a mailing list without your permission.

Client information

If you become a client, your financial records are handled under the terms of your engagement letter and my written information security plan:

  • Records are kept in QuickBooks Online and secure Microsoft cloud storage, not on this website.
  • Documents should be sent through the secure upload link I provide, not by email.
  • Client records are kept for seven years, then securely disposed of.
  • Your information is shared only as needed to provide services, when you authorize it (for example, with your tax preparer), or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics on all websites with Google's browser add-on.
  • Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.
  • Ask about your information. Email me to ask what I have, or to request a correction or deletion, subject to record-keeping requirements.

Links to other sites

This site links to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google, GitHub, and Intuit QuickBooks. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I'm not responsible for how they handle information.

Children

This site is meant for business owners and isn't directed to children under 13. I don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the effective date at the top.
